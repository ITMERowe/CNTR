Privacy

CNTR has no account, server, ads or analytics, and never uses the internet. What you record is stored on this phone and CNTR never sends it anywhere.

If backup is turned on in the phone's settings, Android may include CNTR's data in the phone's own backup to your Google account.

A file leaves the phone only when you export a backup or CSV and choose where to send it.

Uninstalling CNTR or clearing its data deletes everything on the phone, so export a backup regularly.
