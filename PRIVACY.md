# CNTR privacy policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

CNTR is an offline sales counter for booths at conventions and pop-up events. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

## The short version

CNTR does not collect, send or sell any data. Everything you record stays on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where

CNTR stores what you enter: your products (names, prices, costs, colors, payment methods), the sales you record, your expenses, your events and your settings. All of it is kept in the app's private storage on your phone.

- **No internet access:** CNTR doesn't ask for internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no server, no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- **No personal information about your customers** is asked for or recorded; a sale is just a product, a price, a payment method and a time.
- **No other permissions:** CNTR doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or phone identifiers. On Android 10 and older, it asks for storage access only to save backup files to the Documents folder.

## Backups and exports

- **Backup files you create:** *Export backup* and *Export sales as CSV* save a file to `Documents/CNTR` on your phone and open the share sheet. Where the file goes from there (another app, a cloud drive, an email) is your choice and is governed by that service's own policy. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.
- **Automatic backups:** ending an event or a day saves a backup file to `Documents/CNTR/auto` on your phone.
- **Android's own backup:** if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include CNTR's data in your phone's backup to your Google account. This is handled by Android and Google, not by CNTR, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling CNTR or clearing its storage in Android's settings deletes all of its data from the phone. Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them. You can also delete individual products, expenses and past events inside the app.

## Children

CNTR is a business tool and is not directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If CNTR ever starts handling data differently, this policy will be updated first, and the date at the top will change. Past versions are in this file's history on GitHub.
